Book Review: "The Midnight Library" by Matt Haig

Rating: 4.5/5 stars

Matt Haig's "The Midnight Library" is a beautifully written exploration of regret, possibility, and the value of life. The story follows Nora Seed, who finds herself in a magical library between life and death, able to explore alternate versions of her life.

What makes this novel stand out is its philosophical depth wrapped in an accessible, page-turning narrative. Haig manages to tackle themes of depression and existential crisis without ever becoming heavy-handed.

The concept is original and executed brilliantly. Each alternate life Nora explores feels distinct and meaningful. The ending, while somewhat predictable, lands with emotional resonance.

Recommended for: Anyone looking for a thought-provoking but uplifting read.
